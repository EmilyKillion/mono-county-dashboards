    About this sample

    This is a static copy of a weekly respiratory virus surveillance dashboard, showing Mono County data through the end of the 2025-26 season (week of August 31, 2026). Local case, laboratory, hospital, and wastewater data are embedded in the page; CDC wastewater comparisons are retrieved from data.cdc.gov when the page loads and are limited to the same period. In production, the dashboard reads a weekly Excel tracker and is republished each week.

    Mono County Health & Human Services, Public Health Division. Case counts are provisional and may change as reports are received.
    Dashboard developed by Core Epi Solutions
