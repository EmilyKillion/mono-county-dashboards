    About this sample

    This is a static copy of a weekly surveillance dashboard for the 2025-26 respiratory virus season (September 2025 through August 2026). Local case, laboratory, hospital, and wastewater data are embedded in the page; CDC wastewater and test-positivity comparisons are retrieved live from data.cdc.gov when the page loads. In production, the same page reads a weekly Excel tracker and is republished each week.

    Mono County Health & Human Services, Public Health Division. Case counts are provisional and may change as reports are received.
